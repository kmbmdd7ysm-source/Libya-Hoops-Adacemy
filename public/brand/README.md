# LHA brand assets

These are the accepted official Libya Hoops Academy brand assets. Preserve their artwork and stable paths.

- `lha-mark-black.png` — dark mark for light backgrounds.
- `lha-mark-white.png` — light mark for dark backgrounds.
- `lha-wordmark-black.svg` — dark horizontal wordmark.
- `lha-wordmark-white.svg` — light horizontal wordmark.
- `/images/brand/lha-official.png` — official raster brand image.
- `/images/brand/og-image.png` — primary 1200×630 PNG social preview.
- `/favicon.svg` and PWA icons in `/icons/`.

Keep at least 12.5% clear space around the mark. Never mirror, stretch, crop or recolor the artwork.
