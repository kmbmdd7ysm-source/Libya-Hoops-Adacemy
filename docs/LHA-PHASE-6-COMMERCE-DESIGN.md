## Delivered — operational correctness

- `src/utils/commerceGuard.js` provides exact physical, in-stock SKU-type COD eligibility. Eligibility requires a supported physical product, valid SKU and quantity (1–99), at most 25 lines, and a Libya (LY) delivery country.
- `src/pages/CheckoutPage.jsx` shares this eligibility. A cart containing an online training subscription or event registration cannot masquerade as a physical COD delivery. Honest Arabic and English checkout notices identify what payment options are actually supported. Live online payment remains disabled if the payment provider isn't configured; no checkout "paid" success is fabricated.
- `api/order-notification.js` rejects invalid quantities, unsupported COD fulfillment, non-Libya delivery or mismatched payment method **before** initiating Center Vision synchronization. Failed provider details are logged server side but not exposed to buyers in the response. The existing idempotency key flow is preserved.
- `src/pages/CheckoutPage.jsx` saves a local cash order as **pending / unfulfilled**, not as a confirmed/shipping-in-process order. `src/services/orders.js` upgrades to **confirmed** only after a Center Vision acceptance response. `src/components/account/OrderCard.jsx` labels local-only orders as **Pending store confirmation**, not as a final confirmed or dispatched order.
- The actual checkout currency, SKU sale price, inventory availability and deposit policy are **not** altered here. The Center Vision service is still the authoritative commercial backend, not the visitor's browser.
